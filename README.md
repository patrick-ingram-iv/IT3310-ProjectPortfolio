## About This Portfolio

This portfolio contains the project management work I will complete throughout the IT 3310 course. As an Associate Consultant with NovaTech Consulting, I will work through a semester-long client engagement and add each completed project artifact to this repository.

## NovaTech Consulting

NovaTech Consulting is the consulting organization used throughout the course to apply project management concepts to realistic IT situations. My role as an Associate Consultant is to evaluate project information, identify what still needs to be learned, and make recommendations as the engagement moves forward.

## RCHN Engagement

Our client is Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN), a nonprofit health network with six clinics in the Chicago area. RCHN is looking to replace its aging patient intake and scheduling system. The project will develop throughout the semester as we work through project initiation, planning, stakeholder needs, risks, resources, communication, and other areas of project management.
