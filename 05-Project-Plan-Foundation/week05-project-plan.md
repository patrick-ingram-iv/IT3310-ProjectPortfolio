**Consultant:** Patrick Ingram IV
**Role:** Associate Consultant, Project Manager, NovaTech Consulting
**Engagement:** Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN)
**Date:** October 5, 2026
---
## Scope

### In Scope, Phase One
Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics

Appointment scheduling system replacement

Staff training for the new system at all six locations

Initial rollout support for 90 days post-launch

### Out of Scope, Phase One
Patient-facing online scheduling portal

Billing system integration with new scheduling data

Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership

Mobile application for clinic staff

### Scope Justification
Moving the mobile app for clinic staff to OUT OF SCOPE made sense because the project is focused on replacing the current system and addressing scheduling and appointment issues, not mobile applications. Adding a mobile app to the scope now creates unneeded work and takes up money and resources that are better used for the main focus of the project: replacing the scheduling and appointment system across all six clinics.

## SMART Objectives

### Objective 1
By the approved implementation milestone, all six RCHN clinics will use the unified intake and scheduling system to provide a consistent workflow with clear appointment confirmation.

**Connection to Product Vision:** The objective comes from my Week 3 Product Vision because I identified reliability, appointment confirmation, and a consistent workflow across all six clinics as the main needs.

### Objective 2
By the approved rollout milestone, intake coordinators across all six clinics will be able to complete patient intake and scheduling using the standardized workflow.

**Connection to Product Vision:** 
The objective connects with my Week 3 Product Vision by having intake coordinators ready to provide clear appointment confirmation and a consistent workflow across all six clinics.
