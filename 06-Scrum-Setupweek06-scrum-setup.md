## Agile Delivery Fit
I believe Agile delivery would benefit the scheduling and patient intake portions of the RCHN project because it allows the team to develop a working foundation, get feedback from clinic staff, and make changes where necessary. Instead of trying to develop the entire system at once, we can evaluate what works and what needs improvement as the project moves forward.
However, areas such as budgeting, resource availability, and the overall implementation schedule still need stronger advance planning and control. Making changes without considering their effect on cost, time, and resources could create unnecessary problems. I believe a combination of planning and adaptation would work best for this project.
---
## Scrum Team
**Product Owner:** An RCHN representative responsible for the intake and scheduling requirements. This person should understand the needs of the six clinics and determine which features provide the most value.

**Scrum Master:** A NovaTech team member familiar with Scrum. This person would help the team follow Scrum practices, address obstacles, and keep the team working effectively without taking over the Developers' responsibilities.

**Developers:** NovaTech software developers, systems specialists, and testing personnel working with RCHN's technical and clinical workflow experts. They would be responsible for developing, testing, and delivering usable improvements to the system.

### Rationale
These assignments make sense because RCHN understands its operational needs, while NovaTech provides the technical expertise to develop the replacement system. The Scrum Master supports cooperation and helps keep unnecessary obstacles from interfering with the work.

## Sprint 1 Plan
**Sprint Goal:** Establish the basic scheduling and patient intake workflows so they can be reviewed, evaluated, and improved before moving forward with implementation.
**Course Planning Limit:** 25 points

### Selected Backlog Items
- Scheduling Workflow Prototype  High Priority · 13 points   Create a basic prototype of the appointment scheduling workflow for clinic review.
- Intake Form Standardization    High Priority · 8 points    Define the standard intake fields that all six RCHN clinics will use.
- Legacy Data Cleanup Review     Low Priority · 3 points     Review existing intake and scheduling data for obvious quality issues.

**Total Course Planning Points Selected:** 24

## Sprint Planning Reflection
I selected the Scheduling Workflow Prototype, Intake Form Standardization, and Legacy Data Cleanup Review because they help establish the foundation for the new system. The scheduling prototype gives us something to evaluate, standardized intake forms support consistency across all six clinics, and reviewing existing data can help identify problems before they affect the new system.

These three items total 24 points, staying within the 25-point planning limit. I intentionally deferred the Clinic Staff Training Outline because I believe training should be based on a workflow that has already been developed and reviewed. Otherwise, staff could end up learning procedures that constantly change.

The team needs to learn whether the proposed workflow meets the clinics' needs before moving on to additional features. A stable foundation gives us room to make improvements without creating unnecessary confusion or additional work.

---
## Scrum Master Reflection
### Impediment and Support
One impediment could be that the six RCHN clinics have different scheduling procedures and cannot agree on a standardized workflow. This could delay the prototype and affect the Sprint Goal.

The Scrum Master could help by bringing the right people together, making sure the issues are communicated, and helping the team resolve obstacles. However, the Scrum Master should not take over the Developers' work or make decisions for the Product Owner. The goal is to help the team work through the problem while allowing them to manage their own responsibilities.

### Preparing for Week 7
The Sprint Planning activity will help with the Week 7 Product Backlog because we have already started identifying which work is most important, what depends on other work, and what can realistically be completed.

The items we did not select for Sprint 1 still need to be considered and prioritized. We can also use what we learn from the scheduling prototype and clinic feedback to make changes to the Product Backlog. This should help us avoid unnecessary work and keep future Sprints focused on what the project actually needs.
