## User Persona

**Name:** Alfred  
**Role:** Intake Coordinator  
**Years in Role:** 3–7 years  
**Tech Comfort Level:** High tech comfort

---

### Primary Work Goal

Complete patient intake and scheduling accurately and efficiently.

---

### Main Frustration with the Current System

The current system requires too many screens, and staff cannot always confirm whether appointments were saved successfully.

---

### What Would Make Their Work Better

A consistent and reliable intake and scheduling system that clearly confirms saved appointments and supports an efficient workflow across RCHN clinics.

---

## Product Vision Statement

For clinic intake coordinators who need a consistent and reliable intake and scheduling workflow, a unified intake and scheduling system will provide clear appointment confirmation and a consistent, reliable workflow across RCHN clinics.
