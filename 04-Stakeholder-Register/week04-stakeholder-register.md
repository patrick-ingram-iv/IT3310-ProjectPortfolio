## Engagement Strategy Reflection

### Highest Priority Stakeholder

The Clinic Intake Coordinators require the most engagement because they use the system every day. If their concerns are overlooked, the new system could make their jobs harder and lead to problems with adoption. If their job is harder, progress slows and it trickles down to the patients.

### Risk of Omission

I considered community partner organizations but did not include them because they are less involved in the system replacement at this stage. I would add them later if the new system changes how they refer patients or communicate with RCHN.
